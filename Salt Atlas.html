Salt Atlas

Every composition in the master price list, grouped by what it treats. Brands will change as Durgacure takes on new principals; the salts, the strengths and the suffix codes stay the same.

The ending of a salt name tells you its family. Learn these endings and you can place almost every salt in the list before you have memorised a single brand. Tap any salt to open it in the atlas.

The -azole rule

Every -azole kills fungus, except three: -prazole blocks acid (Pantoprazole), -nidazole kills amoeba (Ornidazole, Metronidazole), -bendazole kills worms (Albendazole).

The letters after a brand name are an industry-wide code for the partner salt. Almox-CV, Omee-D, Alkof LS, Fercee XT: a new principal's brand with the same letters will usually carry the same partner. Examples below are from your own list.
