About Us

A modern distribution company for the healthcare ecosystem.

Built with a long-term vision to create a trusted distribution platform for healthcare companies, retailers, institutions and business partners.

DurgaCure LLP operates in the healthcare trade and distribution segment, with activities spanning pharmaceutical formulations, drugs, medicines, nutraceuticals, food and health supplements, cosmetics, surgical products, medical products, diagnostic products and allied healthcare goods.

Our objective is to build efficient supply relationships with manufacturers and principals while providing consistent service to our trade partners.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and scalable healthcare distribution platform known for service quality, reliability and strong channel relationships.
